# Referral Module

Referral portals, partner commissions, payouts, and multi-tenant referral databases are specialized product features. They are intentionally not embedded in the general EC Framework.

Use the separate **Echo Craft Referral SaaS Foundation** package when a project needs:

- business tenants;
- partner applications;
- referral aliases;
- referral visits and leads;
- commissions and payouts; or
- partner dashboards.

The SaaS package can reuse EC Framework's themes, navigation, forms, notifications, administration components, and Supabase conventions.
